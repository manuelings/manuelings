# Hi, I'm Manuel Graterol

I'm a cybersecurity graduate and telecommunications engineer based in Tennessee. I spent nearly three years at HughesNet troubleshooting satellite internet and network connectivity and supporting VSAT field installers.

I earned my B.S. in Cybersecurity at Bellevue University and my telecommunications engineering degree at Universidad Rafael Urdaneta in Venezuela. My cybersecurity coursework included vulnerability assessments, digital forensics, and incident response planning.

I'm building a portfolio focused on security operations and vulnerability management, and I'm open to entry-level cybersecurity roles. I speak Spanish and English.
